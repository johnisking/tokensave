What is a token?

A token is a chunk of text an AI model reads. In English, 1 token is roughly 4 characters or ¾ of a word.

Why do other languages cost more?

Tokenizers are trained mostly on English, so Korean, Japanese, Chinese and others often split into more tokens for the same meaning.

Is my text private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.
