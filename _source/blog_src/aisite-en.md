Free plans and limits change often, so treat "free" below as "you can start without paying" and check each site for today's details.

## Chat assistants

**ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com) is still the place most people start. It handles writing, questions, images, files and voice in one app, and the free plan is enough to learn what AI can do for you.

**Claude** (claude.ai) is the one to try when the text is long: contracts, reports, a whole codebase. It tends to follow detailed instructions closely and say when it isn't sure.

**Gemini** (gemini.google.com) makes the most sense if you live in Google's apps. It can work with your Gmail, Docs and Drive, and it is built into Android.

**Microsoft Copilot** (copilot.microsoft.com) is the equivalent if you live in Windows and Office.

You don't need all of them. Pick one for daily use and keep a second for when the first gets stuck. If you're deciding whether to pay, our [Subscription vs API calculator](/plans) shows whether a $20 plan is worth it for the way you actually use AI.

## Research and learning

**Perplexity** (perplexity.ai) is a search engine that answers in sentences and links every claim to a source. Use it when you need to check facts, not just get an answer.

**NotebookLM** (notebooklm.google.com) answers questions only from documents you give it: PDFs, notes, web pages, YouTube videos. Because it stays inside your sources it is far less likely to make things up, and it can turn a pile of material into a study guide or a short audio overview.

## Writing and translation

**DeepL** (deepl.com) produces translations that read more naturally than most, and it can translate whole Word or PDF files while keeping the layout.

A tip if you write prompts in a language other than English: most AI models charge more tokens for the same request in other languages. Korean costs about 40% more tokens than English and Japanese about 80% more. [TokenSave's token counter](/) can translate a prompt to English on your device and add "Reply in [your language]" so the answer still comes back in your language.

## Images and design

**Canva** (canva.com) is the fastest way to make a thumbnail, slide or social post. Its AI tools can generate images, remove backgrounds and rewrite text inside the design.

**remove.bg** (remove.bg) does one thing: drop in a photo and get the subject cut out in seconds.

If you generate a lot of images, prices per image differ by more than 900% between models. The [AI image cost calculator](/image) compares Nano Banana, GPT Image, FLUX and others side by side.

## Audio, music and video

**ElevenLabs** (elevenlabs.io) turns text into speech that is hard to tell from a real voice. It's popular for narration, dubbing and voiceovers for short videos.

**Suno** (suno.com) writes and sings a complete song from a short description of the style and topic.

**CapCut** (capcut.com) is a free video editor with automatic subtitles, background removal and templates. It is a favorite for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

AI video generation is where costs climb fastest. Before you burn credits, the [AI video cost calculator](/video) shows what the same clip costs on Veo, Kling, Runway and others.

## For tinkerers and developers

**LMArena** (lmarena.ai) lets you send one prompt to two anonymous models and vote for the better answer. The votes feed a public leaderboard that is one of the most-cited rankings of AI models.

**Hugging Face** (huggingface.co) is the home of open AI models. Its Spaces let you try many of them in the browser without installing anything.

**Google AI Studio** (aistudio.google.com) is the quickest way to test Gemini models with your own prompts and get an API key.
