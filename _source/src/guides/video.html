How to use the video cost calculator

  1. Enter the length of each clip in seconds and how many clips you need.
  2. Choose a resolution and whether you need generated audio.
  3. The list ranks every model that offers that combination by total cost, cheapest first.

How prices are calculated

Most video APIs charge per second of output, so the total is seconds × clips × price per second. Luma bills per 5- or 10-second clip instead, and longer videos are counted as several clips. Some models include audio in every clip; others charge more when audio is on. Where a model's maker publishes no per-second API price, we show Runway's API rate for it and label it.

Budget for retries

The calculator shows the cost of one generation. In practice a usable shot often takes two to four attempts, so multiply by about three for a realistic budget, and test prompts on a cheap draft model at 720p before rendering the final take.
