How to use the image cost calculator

  1. Choose the output resolution: 0.5K (512px), 1K (1024px), 2K (2048px) or 4K.
  2. Enter how many images you need.
  3. Every model that supports that size is ranked by total cost.

How prices are calculated

Most image APIs charge a fixed price per image at each resolution. FLUX.2 bills per megapixel: the first megapixel at one rate and each additional one at a lower rate, rounded up. Quality settings matter too: GPT Image 2.5 costs 16 times more at high than at low. Where the maker publishes no direct API price, Runway's API rate is shown and labeled.

Tips

  • Find a composition on a low-quality or small model, then regenerate only the keepers at full quality.
  • For web and social media, 1K is usually enough.
  • Budget two to four attempts for detailed images with hands, text or recurring characters.
