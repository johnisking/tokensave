ChatGPT Go costs $8 a month and ChatGPT Plus costs $20. Since August 2026 both have unlimited everyday text chat, so the old reason to upgrade, running out of messages, is gone. The difference now is which model answers you, how hard it thinks, and what else comes with the plan. Here is what the extra $12 buys and who needs it.

The real difference: Luna vs Sol

Go answers with GPT-5.6 Luna, OpenAI's small, fast model. Plus answers with GPT-5.6 Sol, the mid-size model, and lets you turn up how long it thinks. For quick questions, rewriting a message or summarizing a page, you will rarely notice. For long documents, tricky reasoning, coding and anything where a wrong answer costs you, Sol is clearly stronger.

To put a number on the gap: on the API, Sol-class models cost about 20 times as much per token as Luna-class models. That is roughly what you are paying OpenAI to run for you on Plus.

Who should pick Go

  • You use ChatGPT for everyday questions, writing help and quick summaries.
  • You do not use Codex or ChatGPT Work.
  • You do not mind occasional ads.
  • You want more than the free plan, mainly more memory and room for files, at the lowest price.

Who should pick Plus

  • You write or analyze long documents, or ask questions where accuracy matters.
  • You want the thinking levels for hard problems.
  • You use Codex or ChatGPT Work for coding or agent tasks; Plus is the cheapest plan with real access.
  • You want no ads.

If you hit Plus's limits on Codex or the newest models every week, the next step is Pro 100 at $100. See ChatGPT Pro 100 vs 200 vs 500.

Or skip both and use the API

If you mostly chat, paying per token can be cheaper than either plan. On GPT-6 Luna, a month of 60 messages a day in ongoing conversations costs about a dollar on the API; on GPT-6 Sol the same month is about $20, the price of Plus. You lose the ChatGPT app's extras (images, voice, memory, deep research) and need a third-party chat app. Put in your own usage in the Subscription vs API calculator.
