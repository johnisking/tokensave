Claude Pro is Anthropic's main paid plan, and its price looks simple: $20 a month. What you get for it is less obvious. The usage limits have no fixed message count, there are two separate limits running at once, and Anthropic changed the rules several times in 2026. This guide covers the official price as of October 12, 2026, what Pro includes, how its limits work, whether there is a free trial, and when Max is worth it instead.

How much is Claude Pro?

  • Annual billing saves about 17%. You pay $200 up front instead of $240 over a year.
  • Prices exclude tax. Anthropic's pricing page says applicable tax is added on top. Its help center lists Pro at $20 in the US and £18 in the UK, and shows local currency where it is supported.
  • App store prices can differ. Anthropic's help center says "mobile pricing may vary depending on your app platform," and it doesn't publish the app prices. Compare the price in the app with the web price before subscribing.
  • Max is monthly only. There is no annual discount on either Max plan.
  • Team needs at least 2 seats, up to 150.

What Claude Pro includes

According to Anthropic's pricing page, Pro includes everything in Free plus:

  • More usage than Free. Anthropic says Pro gives at least 500% of Free's usage at peak times.
  • Claude Code, Anthropic's coding agent, in the terminal, IDE and desktop app. Free doesn't include it.
  • All three model families: Opus, Sonnet and Haiku. Free gets Sonnet and Haiku only. Fable is available on Pro only through paid usage credits.
  • Unlimited projects (Free is limited to 5).
  • Research, Claude's multi-step web research mode.
  • Claude in Chrome and Microsoft 365.
  • Dashboards (beta), task handoff and scheduling.

Free already includes memory, connectors, web search, Artifacts, file creation and code execution, so Pro doesn't add those; it gives you more room to use them.

What Pro doesn't include: API usage. The Claude API and Console are billed separately, per token. See How much does a Claude token cost?

Claude Pro usage limits

Pro has two limits running at the same time:

Limit How it works
Session limit Resets every 5 hours
Weekly limit Applies across all models; resets weekly (Settings > Usage)
  • There is no fixed message count. How far a limit goes depends on the model, the length of your conversations, attached files and whether you use Claude Code.
  • No fixed message count: Anthropic's help center does not give a number of messages per 5 hours. Longer chats, big files and Opus use the limit much faster.
  • The Claude app and Claude Code share the same limits. A long coding session leaves less for chat, and the other way around.
  • You can keep going past a limit by turning on paid usage credits.
  • Anthropic may add monthly caps on top, according to its help center.

What changed in 2026

  • May 6, 2026: Anthropic raised Claude Code's 5-hour limits by 100% for Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise, and removed the extra limit reduction during peak hours for Pro and Max.
  • May 13 to July 19, 2026: Claude Code weekly limits were 50% higher for paid plans. That promotion has ended.

Is there a Claude Pro free trial?

Not right now. Anthropic's pricing page doesn't offer a trial, and its promotions page lists only two offers, both expired:

  • One free month of Pro for new sign-ups with a work email, October 29–30, 2025.
  • One free month of Max for returning Max subscribers, October 29 to November 1, 2025.

Pages that promise a Claude Pro free trial are usually describing those 2025 offers. Anthropic's help center says there are no standing discounts. We found no official student discount either.

The closest thing to a trial is the Free plan: it has Sonnet, Haiku, projects, memory and web search, so you can see whether Claude suits you before paying. What you can't test on Free is Claude Code or Opus.

Can you get a refund?

  • Generally no. Anthropic's help center says payments are non-refundable except where its terms or the law say otherwise.
  • EEA and UK users can cancel within 14 days for a refund, reduced in proportion to what they used.
  • iPhone subscriptions are refunded through Apple, not Anthropic. Android refund requests go to Anthropic Support.
  • Moving to a Team plan cancels your Pro or Max subscription with a prorated refund.

How to subscribe and cancel

  • Subscribe at claude.ai/upgrade on the web, or through the Claude app on iPhone or Android.
  • Cancel on the web or desktop: Settings, then Billing, then Cancel.
  • Cancel in the mobile app: tap your initials, then Billing, then Manage subscription.
  • Timing: cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Is Claude Pro worth it?

  • Yes, if you use Claude most days. Pro gives at least 500% of Free's usage and adds Opus, Claude Code and Research.
  • Yes, if you want to try Claude Code. Pro is the cheapest way in.
  • Probably not, if you chat a few times a week. The Free plan may be enough.
  • Not, if you only need the API. Pro doesn't include API usage; pay per token instead. (Max, by contrast, lists $100 or $200 a month in API credits on the pricing page.)
  • Consider Max if you code with Claude for hours every day and keep hitting Pro's 5-hour limit.

My own path

I started on the Free plan, moved to Pro, and now use Max 5x. I work in the Claude desktop app on one PC. Coding work and tasks that use external MCP tools used a lot of tokens, so for that kind of work Max felt like the better fit than Pro. If you only need other kinds of work, Pro felt like enough. When Opus 5 was the main model, I used up my limit. Since Opus 5.5, I haven't hit it, although one recent week reached 93% of the weekly limit.

Verdict

  • Claude Pro costs $20 a month, or $17 a month billed annually, plus tax.
  • It adds Claude Code, Opus, Research and more usage to the Free plan, with no fixed message count.
  • There is no free trial right now; the 2025 free-month offers have expired. The Free plan is the way to try Claude first.
  • Upgrade to Max only if you hit Pro's limits most weeks; Max 20x is the only plan with a lower price per unit of usage.

FAQ

How much is Claude Pro per month? $20 a month in the US with monthly billing, or $17 a month billed annually ($200 a year), plus applicable tax. In the UK it is £18 a month plus tax.

Does Claude Pro have a free trial? Not currently. The last official free-month offers ran in October and November 2025. You can use the Free plan to try Claude.

Does Claude Pro include Claude Code? Yes. Claude Code is included with Pro and shares the same usage limits as the Claude app.

How many messages do you get with Claude Pro? There is no fixed number. Anthropic says it depends on message length, files, conversation length and the model; longer conversations and Opus use the limit faster. How resets work is in Claude usage limits.

Is Claude Pro cheaper on the app? Not necessarily. Anthropic says mobile pricing may vary by app platform, so the price in the iPhone or Android app can differ from the web price. Check both before subscribing.

Is Claude Pro or Max better for Claude Code? Pro is enough to try Claude Code and use it for occasional tasks. If you code with it for hours most days and keep hitting the 5-hour limit, Max 5x gives 500% of Pro's usage for $100 a month.

Can I cancel Claude Pro anytime? Yes. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in Settings, then Billing. You keep access until the end of the paid period. Refunds are generally not given except where the law requires, such as the 14-day window in the EEA and UK.
