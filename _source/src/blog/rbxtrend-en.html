What each formula is, and why it works

Steal a ___. Everyone has a base full of things that earn money; you can run into other players' bases and carry their best items home. It mixes a tycoon (your base grows while you are away) with risk and revenge, so players come back to defend and to get even. The noun in the title changes with whatever is popular this month: brainrot characters, eggs, animals.

Co-op horror. Small groups survive something together: a monster, a night in the woods, a maze of rooms. Fear is more fun with friends, and every round plays out differently, which keeps it on streams and in videos.

1v1 duels. Short, skill-based fights with a clear winner. Rounds last a minute or two, so "one more" is easy, and ranks and weapon skins give a reason to keep playing.

+1 per second. A stat goes up by 1 every second (speed, size, jump height) and you use it to get further in an escape course or a race. Progress is visible every second, and it costs very little to build.

RNG / luck collecting. Roll, fish or open things for a chance at something rare. The slim odds of a rare drop are the whole game; collections and trading do the rest. Fishing has become the most popular theme.

Pets. Raise, ride, trade and show off pets. The oldest formula on Roblox and still one of the biggest: when we checked, Adopt Me! had more players than any game on this list except Steal An Egg.

Simple-action sims. One silly, satisfying action repeated with upgrades: peel a potato, melt ice, drill, build a pyramid. The title is the whole pitch, which makes them easy to click on and quick to make.

2-player co-op obby. An obstacle course you can only finish with a partner: carry something together, share a cart, or stay chained to each other. Made for playing with a friend and for clips of both of you failing.

Which one should you make first?

If it is your first Roblox game, start with +1 per second, a simple-action sim or a 2-player obby. They need little art, few systems and no balancing of player-vs-player fights, and our calculator puts a small one at about 1–2 weeks solo with AI tools.

Steal a ___, RNG and pets sell well through game passes and developer products, but you need saving, a shop and some care with fairness. Co-op horror and duels take the most work to get right: horror needs atmosphere and level design; duels need smooth combat and fair matchmaking.

Whatever you pick, put your own twist on the formula instead of copying a hit. The noun in "Steal a ___" or the action in a simple sim is where your idea goes.

What it costs to make one

Publishing on Roblox is free and Roblox runs the servers, so the cost is mostly your AI tools. For a small game:

Stack Cost
Cheapest: Gemini, free audio, Google AI Pro, Roblox Assistant about $25–40
Typical: Midjourney, Suno, ElevenLabs, Claude Max 5× about $110–150

Most of the money is the AI coding plan. Roblox Assistant's 3D model generation is free in Studio, with daily limits.
