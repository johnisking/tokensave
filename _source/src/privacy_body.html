Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information TokenSave (tokensave.app) collects and how it is used.

Text you enter

All calculations run in your browser. Text you type or paste into the token counter is never sent to our servers or to any third party, and is not stored.

Settings saved on your device

If you choose a language, it is saved in your browser's local storage so the site can remember it. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across websites. It collects basic, non-identifying information such as the page visited, referrer, country and browser type.

We also use Google Tag Manager, which may load Google Analytics. Google Analytics may set cookies to measure visits and how pages are used. What you type into the tools is never sent to it: it stays in your browser. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Third-party resources

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a tokenizer library from the jsDelivr CDN. These providers receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: contact@jonhisking.com.
