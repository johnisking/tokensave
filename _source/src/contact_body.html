Contact

TokenSave is built and run by Jonhisking, an independent developer based in Seosan, South Korea. Every message is read by a person, usually within two working days.

contact@jonhisking.com

What to write about

  • A price is wrong or out of date. Tell us the model and, if you can, link the provider's pricing page. Prices are checked by hand, so corrections are very welcome.
  • A model is missing. Name the model and where its API price is published.
  • A translation reads badly. The site is in 41 languages. If a sentence in your language sounds wrong, send the page address and a better wording.
  • Something is broken. Tell us the page, your browser and what you expected to happen.
  • Privacy questions or requests. See the privacy policy, then email us with anything it does not answer.
  • Press and partnerships. Happy to talk. Please include a short description of what you have in mind.

Before you write

Please do not send the text you are counting. The tools run in your browser and we never need to see your prompts to help you.
